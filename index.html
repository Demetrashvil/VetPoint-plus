Frequently askedquestions

Everything you need to know before getting started with VetPoint+.

Is VetPoint+ a replacement for a veterinarian?

+

No. VetPoint+ provides AI-powered guidance and helps you better understand your pet's symptoms, but it does not replace professional veterinary care.

How accurate is the AI assistant?

+

Our AI analyzes the information you provide and offers educational guidance based on trusted veterinary knowledge. For emergencies, always contact a veterinarian immediately.

Can I store my pet's medical records?

+

Yes. You can keep vaccinations, medications, laboratory results and veterinary visits organized in one secure place.

Is my data secure?

+

Absolutely. We use modern security practices to protect your personal information and your pet's medical history.

Is VetPoint+ free to use?

+

Yes. You can get started for free, with additional premium features becoming available in the future.
